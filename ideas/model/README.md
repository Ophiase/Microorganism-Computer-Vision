# ideas/model/: what a trajectory is

The mathematics under the pipeline, one concept per file, read in order.

1. [trajectory.md](trajectory.md): the hidden state of one cell, and the
   chain through which the video shows it.
2. [representation.md](representation.md): a shape seen in the plane, as a
   point, an oriented point or a skeleton, and why the filament.
3. [models.md](models.md): the basic laws of motion, nested, from Brownian
   to run-and-tumble and the body wave of a worm.
4. [interactions.md](interactions.md): laws that depend on the neighbours
   and the decor.
5. [internal-state.md](internal-state.md): a hidden state inside the cell
   that steers its behaviour.
6. [population.md](population.md): many cells, and surrogates as the null
   of an interaction.
7. [hypotheses.md](hypotheses/README.md): each hypothesis a pair of nested models,
   its test, and whether the test is trusted yet.
