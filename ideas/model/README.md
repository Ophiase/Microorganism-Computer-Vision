# ideas/model/: what a trajectory is

The mathematics under the pipeline, one concept per file, read in order.

1. [trajectory.md](trajectory.md): the state of one cell, and what the
   video shows of it.
2. [population.md](population.md): many cells, the decor, the others, and
   surrogates as the null of an interaction.
3. [models.md](models.md): the laws of motion, nested, from Brownian to
   run-and-tumble and the body wave of a worm.
4. [hypotheses.md](hypotheses.md): each hypothesis a pair of nested models,
   its test, and whether the test is trusted yet.
