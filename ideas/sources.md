# Other sources

**[question]** Today the pipeline reads one study: nine videos of Salmonella
on figshare, listed in `settings/dataset.toml`. Look later at other public
sources of bacterial motility video — other species, other microscopes, other
frame rates — so that a verdict is not a property of one dataset.

- A source is one settings file, never code: an address, the file ids, and the
  pixel size and frame rate it was filmed at ([open](open.md), question 3).
- A source with tracked ground truth is worth more than ten without: it feeds
  the benchmark of [science](science.md) step 1.

  datasets of [science](science.md) are the first places to look.
